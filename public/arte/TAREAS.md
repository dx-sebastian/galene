## El contrato nuevo — va incluido en cada prompt, no hay que pegarlo

Antes yo pedía la imperfección **por lista**: grafito visible, backruns,
pasajes levantados, salpicaduras. Eso es lo más impuesto que existe — el
modelo coloca accidentes como quien coloca adornos.

El contrato nuevo pide **velocidad, economía y no corregir**. La
imperfección aparece sola, como consecuencia de pintar rápido y parar
antes de tiempo. Es la diferencia entre un cuadro que *tiene* accidentes
y un cuadro que *los sufrió*.

## 1 · Manglar enterrado

- [ ] `manglar-enterrado-a.png`

El manglar de ahora está apoyado sobre la línea del agua como una calca.
Necesito que **las raíces sigan por debajo**, para poder hundirlo: se ve
el agua cortándolas a media altura, no posándose sobre ellas.

```
A watercolour study on rough cotton paper, painted fast in one sitting
and left alone. Wet, generous, economical: few strokes, none of them
corrected. Whatever went wrong stays. The paper shows through wherever
the brush skipped.

SUBJECT — one small red mangrove standing IN water, seen from the side.

· The CROWN takes the top 55%: a rounded, lopsided mass of foliage,
  wider than tall, painted as one soft wash with three or four darker
  strokes inside. Never individual leaves.
· The PROP ROOTS take the bottom 45%: eight or ten thin roots fanning
  out from a short trunk in a wide skirt, some crossing.
· CRITICAL: the roots CONTINUE PAST THE BOTTOM EDGE of the frame. They
  are not cropped at a waterline — they carry on down and out of the
  picture, so the tree can be sunk to any depth. Paint the lower fifth of
  the roots slightly paler and softer, as things look underwater.
· Roughly as wide as tall. Modest, never tall and spindly.

COLOUR: a single mix of Payne's grey and raw umber for the roots; the
crown a cool grey-green, only slightly greener and the same value.
Muted, mineral, alive — not grey, not saturated.

Flat even diffuse light. No sun, no cast shadow, no ground, no bank, no
rocks, no birds, no nest, no sky, no horizon, no reflection.
Transparent background, clear margin left and right. Portrait 2:3.

NEGATIVE: cropped roots, flat bottom edge, tree on a shore, botanical
illustration, photorealistic, 3D render, bark texture, outlines, vector,
saturated green, symmetrical, finished, polished, tidy.
```

## 3 · La gaviota posada

- [ ] `gaviota-posada.png`

Cambiamos de ave. Tú la has llamado gaviota tres veces, y tienes razón:
una gaviota se posa donde sea, es compacta y se lee a tamaño pequeño.
Una garza es alta, fina y se pierde.

```
A watercolour study on rough cotton paper, painted in under two minutes
and left alone. Four or five strokes, none corrected. Bare paper wherever
the brush skipped.

SUBJECT — a single gull perched, strict side profile facing left, at rest.
Compact body, folded wings, short legs, small hooked beak. Head slightly
sunk into the shoulders, the way a gull stands when it is not doing
anything. It is a GESTURE, NOT A CHARACTER: no eye, no face, no feather
detail. If it looks friendly, it is wrong.

The body is one loaded stroke: darker where the pigment pooled under the
belly, almost bare paper across the back. One edge dries hard, the other
dissolves into nothing.

COLOUR: cool grey with a trace of raw umber; the back a shade deeper.
Mineral, muted, alive.

Feet cropped exactly at the bottom edge — that edge is where it stands.
Transparent background. No perch, no water, no shadow, no reflection,
no background, no other birds. The bird fills 85% of the frame height.
Portrait 2:3. Flat even diffuse light.

NEGATIVE: cute, cartoon, character, eye, beak detail, seagull mascot,
storybook, outlined, vector, 3D, photorealistic, saturated, finished.
```

## 6 · El agua, con más pigmento

- [ ] `mar-cercano-vivo.png` (4:1 apaisada, **2508×627** exactos)

El mar de ahora es correcto y está apagado. Este es el mismo mar pintado
con más agua y más pigmento, sin subir la saturación: el color viene de
la separación del granulado, no de un tinte más fuerte.

```
A watercolour study on rough cotton paper, painted very wet and very
fast, in one pass, and abandoned. No correction, no second layer over
anything that dried. Where two washes met while wet they bloomed and it
was left that way.

SUBJECT — a horizontal band of calm sea close to the viewer, at eye level
just above the surface. Long low swells as overlapping wet washes. Large
areas of bare paper for the brightest facets.

COLOUR — this is the point: heavily GRANULATING pigments that separate on
the paper, so the wash is never one flat colour. Cerulean blue, Payne's
grey and a touch of raw umber, dropped in wet so they settle apart and
you can see the grains of each. The overall impression stays cool and
muted — the life comes from the separation, NOT from stronger colour.

Mostly quiet: two or three passages where almost nothing happens.
Seamless left and right edges. Aspect ratio 4:1.
No sky, no horizon, no objects, no foam, no sparkle, no sunlight.

NEGATIVE: flat colour, digital gradient, uniform texture, machine-regular
ripples, photorealistic, 3D render, saturated, turquoise, tropical,
glossy, HDR, sunlight on water, finished, polished.
```

## 7 · El cielo, con más color

- [ ] `nubes-color.png` (3:2, transparente)

```
A watercolour study on rough cotton paper, painted wet and fast, and
abandoned. MOSTLY EMPTY PAPER — the void is the subject.

SUBJECT — three or four loose horizontal cloud washes drifting across an
otherwise bare sheet. Each is one wet stroke: dissolved at one end,
ending in a hard dried edge at the other. No cloud has a defined shape.

COLOUR — pale but NOT colourless. Where the washes overlap, a faint warm
blush of raw sienna sits against the cool grey-blue, the way real cloud
does near the horizon. Granulating pigments that separate visibly.
At least 70% of the sheet untouched.

Transparent background. No sky colour behind, no horizon, no sun, no
rays, no glow, no land, no birds. Landscape 3:2.

NEGATIVE: fluffy clouds, cumulus, defined shapes, outlines, cotton wool,
sunset, golden hour, rays, glow, storybook, decorative, vector, 3D,
photorealistic, saturated.
```
